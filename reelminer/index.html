You save reels because they have something in them. Then they sit there. Reel Miner transcribes every reel you saved, sorts them into categories, writes the checklist, and answers your questions from them.

No spam. One email when it opens, and founding members keep the £5 price for good.

How it works

1. Hand over your saves

Instagram lets you download everything you saved in one file. Drop it in, or share reels one at a time.

2. Every reel gets read

Transcript, on-screen text and caption become one note: what it claims, what to do, the numbers, the tools named, and whether it was worth your time.

3. Click a category

Categories come from what your reels actually say. Each one has a topline summary, a checklist you can tick, and the reels behind it.
